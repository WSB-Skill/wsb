# Should I get a dog

Unedited output from a test run with the skill loaded. Questions that aren't about money get priced as trades.

---

*Claude Sonnet*

**User:** /wsb should i get a dog

**Agent:**

Sir, you're asking a language model whether to take on a 12-year position with no exit.

A dog is a long-dated short on your own free time. Carry is negative every day: food, vet bills, the 6am walk in January. There's no secondary market, so closing early costs you the one thing you're trying to keep. Theta is your weekends, and it never lets you roll.

The adult rescue is the sensible structure. It's housebroken, the temperament is known, and the vol has already been priced. It's fine, and it's boring.

The upside is also real, which is the annoying part. It's the only asset that's pleased to see you when you're down 90%. You were always going to do this, you regard.

**The play:** puppy, eight weeks old. It's the far OTM ticket: nobody knows what it grows into, and all the value is in the tail. Get the one that looks like it's already plotting something.
**What kills it:** the couch, the deposit, and three months without sleep. Mostly the couch.

Not financial advice. It's a lease on your life, and the landlord is a labrador.
